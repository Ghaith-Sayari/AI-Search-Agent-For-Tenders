# Accepted countries and sectors

Policy version: 1.0

## Current default

No country, region, or procurement sector is currently excluded by policy.
The Gatekeeper must therefore not reject a legitimate active tender solely
because of its country or sector.

## Future configuration

When commercial scope is defined, maintain the following lists in this file:

- supported countries;
- supported procurement sectors;
- excluded countries or regions; and
- excluded sectors.

Until those lists are explicitly approved, an unfamiliar country or sector is
not a rejection reason. If it cannot be identified, retain the record's raw
value and use `needs_review` only when that missing context prevents a reliable
tender decision.
